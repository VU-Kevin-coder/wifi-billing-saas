# Subscription billing: isolated foundation, NOT production-ready

Checkout is default-off (`SUBSCRIPTION_CHECKOUT_ENABLED=false`). The only registered adapter is `UnavailableGateway`, which cannot send or verify anything, even if the flag is enabled. The fake adapter exists only in tests. No production provider contract was established: the existing OBPay/PawaPay code is coupled to other payment workflows, and its exact authenticated status response, terminal semantics, amount/currency/reference guarantees and subscription callback routing have not been independently established. Do not connect a provider or enable checkout until those contracts, credentials, reconciliation procedures and security are independently reviewed and tested.

The OBPay subscription adapter candidate is deliberately disabled and unbound: it performs no requests and cannot verify payment. `CollectionPurposeRouter` and `IsolatedCollectionMappingStore` are isolated prototypes, not integrated with checkout, callbacks, voucher payments, or fulfillment. Their schema is a test fixture under `tests/fixtures/migrations`, not an application migration. Neither prototype establishes safe shared-account processing; account-bound provider verification, atomic mapping creation, immutable mappings, production database concurrency tests, secure handler integration, and operational recovery remain prerequisites. Do not deploy the fixture or use these prototypes for real payments.

Assumptions: manual one-month renewal of the currently assigned plan only; current catalog price in whole UGX, no rounding; unsupported currency or fractional amounts fail closed. An encrypted, tenant-bound quote expires after 15 minutes and requires explicit confirmation; changed or tampered prices fail closed. The term starts at the later of now, the current end date, or the remaining trial end for a trial, with calendar-month no-overflow arithmetic. Not recurring. Suspended tenants and changed plans require human review and are not automatically reactivated. Subscription status is not evidence of historic payment; old or manual payments are not imported. Wallet/voucher flows are separate.

The application creates a tenant-scoped invoice, one durable attempt, and insert-only application history snapshots before sending. An uncertain submission is never retried automatically. Only an authenticated adapter lookup with exact reference, integer amount and currency can transition to paid; duplicates do not extend twice. Paid while suspended or after plan change becomes review. Failed attempts and unresolved invoices block another payment and currently need operational intervention. This is conservative, not a complete reconciliation workflow. No production adapter, webhook, scheduler, refunds, dispute handling, admin resolution UI, recovery after process crash, retry/new attempt, plan change checkout or migration of old payment history is provided. DB users can still mutate history: append-only is a convention in this code, not a database-enforced immutable audit log. Invoice/event pagination is provided.

Before use: unify platform manual administration with the same tenant lock, invoice/event ledger and review process (the existing manual controller writes subscription state directly); independently design secure reconciliation and recovery for lost starts, late payments, duplicate/provider terminal-state inconsistencies, refunds and disputes; review provider identity/authentication/contract, security, authorization, audit, access controls, deployment and rollback; add real concurrent transaction tests on the production engine and integration/e2e tests without real money. SQLite sequential tests do not establish row-lock or exactly-once behavior under concurrency. Enable only after those gaps are resolved. Do not treat this patch as financial or production approval.
